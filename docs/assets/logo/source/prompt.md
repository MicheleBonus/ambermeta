# Prompt for the image model

The prompt drafted for the design. If the wording you finally used differed, replace it here.

```
Design a flat vector wordmark logo that reads exactly "AmberMeta": one word, nine letters,
A-m-b-e-r-M-e-t-a, capital A and capital M. It is the logo of a scientific software tool that
turns molecular dynamics simulation files into checked, well-documented records. Spell it exactly
as given; no other text.

The two capital letters are stylized and carry the idea:
- The capital "A" is a drop of amber, the fossil resin that preserves things unchanged: a smooth
  teardrop standing on its round base with its point at the top, forming the apex of the "A".
  Its crossbar is a short straight line ending in two small solid circles, like a tiny chain
  preserved inside the amber. One small white crescent highlight on the upper left of the drop.
- The capital "M" is drawn as a connected chain: five small solid circles at the five corners
  of the letter, joined by straight strokes of uniform width, like a simple workflow graph.

The remaining letters "mber" and "eta" are in a clean, geometric, slightly rounded sans-serif,
medium weight, with the same stroke width as the "M", so that the whole word looks like one
consistent piece. Comfortable letter spacing; the stylized capitals must still read clearly
as letters.

Colors (exactly these, nothing else):
- "Amber": amber #E69F00, with a flat darker amber #C27C00 on the right side of the drop only
- "Meta": dark slate #1F2D3A
- white highlight; plain white background

Style: flat vector, solid fills, no gradients, no shadows, no glow, no texture, no 3D, no
outlines, no photorealism, no decorative swooshes. Horizontal layout, centered, wide canvas.
Avoid DNA helices, atom orbit symbols, ball-and-stick clusters, and any resemblance to the logo
of the AMBER molecular dynamics package.
```

The model shifted the colors slightly; the SVGs use the colors measured from its image
(`#E8A00A`, `#CC8404`, `#243442`).
